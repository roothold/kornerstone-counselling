How it works

Three simple steps to your first session

1

Book a slot

Pick a time that fits your week. First-time visitors start with a free 15-minute intake call so we can meet and match you with the right counsellor.

2

Share your story

In your session, you talk — we listen. Everything you share is held in strict confidence. There's no judgement, no rush, no scripts.

3

Walk forward together

We agree on a path — how often to meet, what to work on, what Scripture and practice to lean into. You set the pace; we walk with you.

Our approach

Rooted in Scripture. Guided by the Spirit. Grounded in real-world care.

We believe healing is holistic — it happens when the Word of God, the presence of the Holy Spirit, and skilled human care meet in one room. Our counsellors are trained professionals whose first allegiance is to Christ, whose posture is confidential and compassionate, and whose method is thoughtful, not formulaic.

You will not be preached at. You will be prayed with, listened to, and walked with — for as long as it takes to find your footing again.
