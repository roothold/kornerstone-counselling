Marriage

Marriage Counselling

Whether you're in a season of crisis, quiet drift, or intentional renewal — marriage counselling gives you a room designed for the two of you to be honest, be heard, and be helped.

What we address: communication breakdown, trust after breach, intimacy struggles, in-law dynamics, money conflict, blended-family tension, parenting disagreements, and the slow drift that couples often can't name.

How it works: most couples start with weekly 90-minute sessions for the first 6-8 weeks, then move to fortnightly. Sessions can be together or occasionally separately when helpful.

Family

Family Counselling

Family is where our earliest wounds and our deepest belonging both live. When those two collide, family counselling helps everyone find their footing again.

What we address: parent-teen conflict, sibling estrangement, generational patterns of hurt, blended-family adjustment, grief in the family system, aging-parent dynamics, and helping a family recover after crisis.

How it works: family sessions can include the whole family together, subsets (e.g. one parent + one child), or individual sessions for one family member seeking peace when others aren't ready. We start where you can start.

Book Family Counselling
Pre-Marital

Pre-Marital Counselling

The best marriages don't happen by accident — they're built on conversations most couples never have before the wedding. Pre-marital counselling gives you those conversations, guided.

What we cover: expectations of marriage, communication styles, conflict patterns from your families of origin, money and lifestyle values, children and parenting, sexual intimacy, spiritual leadership in the home, and how to keep growing together after the honeymoon.

How it works: six structured 90-minute sessions across 8-12 weeks. Most couples finish saying, "we thought we knew each other — we know each other so much better now."

Book Pre-Marital Counselling
Individual

Individual Counselling

Sometimes the season you're in is yours to carry, and you need a private, safe place to unpack it — with someone who listens well, prays well, and knows Scripture.

What we address: anxiety and depression, grief and loss, identity and calling, past trauma, relational hurt, addictive patterns, spiritual dryness, life transitions, and the ache of feeling stuck.

How it works: 60-minute sessions, weekly at first and then shaped to what you need. Fully confidential. Available in person in Lagos or online worldwide.

Book Individual Counselling
Ministry & Leadership

Ministry & Leadership Counselling

Pastors, ministry leaders, and Christian professionals carry burdens few others see. This is a strictly confidential space to process, recover, and lead from wholeness — not empty.

What we address: burnout and compassion fatigue, difficult decisions, team and board conflict, moral wounds and failure recovery, isolation, the tension between calling and family, and the specific spiritual warfare that comes with leadership.

How it works: monthly or fortnightly 60-90 minute sessions, structured around the rhythms of your ministry season. Strict confidentiality — we understand what's at stake.

Book Leadership Counselling
