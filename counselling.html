Counselling pathways

Choose the pathway that fits your season.

Three focused pathways, each with the same commitments: strict confidentiality, Scripture-anchored care, and a counsellor who listens more than they talk. Every pathway starts with a short interest form so we can place you with the right counsellor.

Home / Counselling

Individual

Individual Counselling

Sometimes the season you’re in is yours to carry, and you need a private, safe place to unpack it, with someone who listens well, prays well, and knows Scripture.

What we address: anxiety and depression, grief and loss, identity and calling, past trauma, relational hurt, addictive patterns, spiritual dryness, life transitions, and the ache of feeling stuck.

How it works: start by completing the short interest form below. Your responses go straight to our counselling team, and we’ll follow up to guide you through the next steps.

Complete the Interest Form
Pre-Marital

Pre-Marital Counselling

The best marriages don’t happen by accident, they’re built on conversations most couples never have before the wedding. Pre-marital counselling gives you those conversations, guided.

What we cover: expectations of marriage, communication styles, conflict patterns from your families of origin, money and lifestyle values, children and parenting, sexual intimacy, spiritual leadership in the home, and how to keep growing together after the honeymoon.

How it works: start by completing the short interest form below. Your responses go straight to our counselling team, and we’ll follow up to schedule your first session together.

Complete the Interest Form
Couples

Couples Counselling

Whether you’re in a season of crisis, quiet drift, or intentional renewal, couples counselling gives you a room designed for the two of you to be honest, be heard, and be helped.

What we address: communication breakdown, trust after breach, intimacy struggles, in-law dynamics, money conflict, blended-family tension, parenting disagreements, and the slow drift many couples can’t name.

How it works: start by completing the short interest form below, either of you can fill it. Your responses go straight to our counselling team, and we’ll follow up to schedule your first session together.

Complete the Interest Form
